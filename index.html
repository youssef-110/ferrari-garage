458 Spider

Built from 2011 to 2015. A naturally aspirated 4.5-litre V8 behind the seats, and an aluminium roof that folds away in 14 seconds.

Shaped by air.

Small winglets inside the front intakes flex at speed, lowering drag without a single motor.

Stops as hard as it goes.

Carbon-ceramic brakes come standard. They shrug off the heat that would fade steel discs.

One line, nose to tail.

Pininfarina drew it. Under the paint, an aluminium spaceframe keeps the dry weight to 1,430 kg.

9,000 rpm.

The F136 V8 makes 570 cv at nine thousand revs, and breathes out through three tailpipes grouped in the centre of the diffuser.
